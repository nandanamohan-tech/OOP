Food Inventory Management System

Abstract

The Food Inventory System is a Java-based application designed to simplify the process ofmanaging and
monitoring food items stored at home, stores or restaurans. People often forget about stored food items,
purchase duplicates, or fail to use products before their expiry dates. The traditional method of manually
keeping track of food items can be inconvenient, time-consuming, and may lead to unnecessary food
wastage.
This system provides a centralized platform where users can add, update, remove, and view food items in
theirinventory.Userscanenter detailssuchasfood name,category,quantity,purchasedate,and expirydate.
The system can track expiry information and identify items that are nearing their expiry date, helping users
prioritize foodconsumptionandmakebetterpurchasingdecisions.
The application aims to provide an organized method of managing household food inventory, reduce
unnecessarypurchases,minimize foodwastage, andmake it easierforusers tokeep track of their available
fooditems.
